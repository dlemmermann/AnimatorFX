# Code of Conduct

AnimatorFX welcomes contributors regardless of background, experience or identity. This policy
applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces.

Be respectful, assume good intent, provide constructive technical feedback and respect privacy.
Disagreements are welcome; personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
conduct and publishing another person's private information are not.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, pause a discussion, warn participants or
restrict participation. Actions should be proportionate to the conduct and its impact.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the
[DLSC contact page](https://www.dlsc.com/contact/). Include links and context where possible;
do not post private details in public issues. Reports will be treated confidentially to the
extent possible, reviewed fairly, and acted on without retaliation against good-faith reporters.
